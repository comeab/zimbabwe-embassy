Redesign proposal · Welcome animation

Three ways to say “welcome”

Each concept opens the site with the Ambassador's welcome and shows Zimbabwe's link to Sweden, Norway, Denmark and Finland. All three are built from code (no video file), so they load fast and stay sharp on any screen.

  • Design proposal, not the official Embassy website
  • Plays once per visitor
  • “Skip” always visible
  • Under 10 seconds
  • Respects reduced-motion settings
  • Works in portrait on phones
Concept A · with the thread from B

AOne embassy, four countries

The idea

The Nordic map holds still. A timeline runs from 1980: as each country establishes relations with Zimbabwe it lights up and says welcome in its language. When the Embassy opens, a thread in Zimbabwe's colours stitches Harare to Stockholm. It ends on the Ambassador's handwritten welcome, then the whole layer fades away to reveal the homepage.

Country colours

Gold: one colour for one embassy, with each flag shown in its label. Zimbabwe colours: the four countries take the green, gold, red and white of Zimbabwe's flag. Flags: each country filled with its own flag.

To confirm with the Embassy

The year the Embassy opened in Stockholm (dashed placeholder), and any other milestones for the timeline. Relations: Sweden, Norway, Denmark 18 April 1980; Finland 1 August 1980. Photo: Reinis Inkēns / Saeima, CC BY-SA 2.0.

“I hope this website will serve you as a useful resource, as well as a virtual connection to our homeland.”

Enter the website →

The idea

The flag of Zimbabwe slides in from the left, the four Nordic flags answer from the right, one after another. A golden thread stitches the seam between them. The flags dim and a personal card rises, with “Welcome” written by hand.

What it says

Two peoples sewn together: partnership and friendship. Warm and personal, the closest in tone to a letter from the Ambassador.

Build

~7 s. Pure CSS and SVG with the official flag artwork; under 10 KB. The easiest of the three to maintain.

Concept C

CThe Great House of Stone, under northern lights

Enter the website →

The idea

“Zimbabwe” means great house of stone. A dry-stone wall with the chevron frieze of the Great Enclosure rises under the northern lights. Its door opens, warm light spills out and the Ambassador stands in the doorway as the greeting cycles through six languages.

What it says

Zimbabwe's house, here in the North, with its door open to you. The most cinematic and symbolic of the three, and the strongest brand moment.

Build

~8 s. CSS and a few lines of script, no libraries, under 15 KB. Best with a cut-out portrait of the Ambassador standing (a short photo session).
