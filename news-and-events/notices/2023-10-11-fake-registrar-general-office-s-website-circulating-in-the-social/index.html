Warning: a fake Registrar General’s Office website is circulating on social media

News & events

  • Overview
  • News
  • Agenda
  • Notices
  • Gallery

This notice ended on 11 April 2024 and is kept for reference.

All Zimbabwean nationals

RE: FAKE REGISTRAR GENERAL OFFICE’S WEBSITE CIRCULATING IN THE SOCIAL MEDIA

The above captioned subject matter refers:

Please be advised that there is a fake Registrar General’s Office Website (www.makombeofficial.org) circulating in the social media purporting to provide the general Zimbabwean public at home and abroad, a facility to apply for all registry documents such as Birth Certificates, National Registration Cards and Passports online. The website appears so authentic and interactive that it can easily deceive even a discerning eye.

ZIMBABWE EMBASSY – STOCKHOLM

11th October 2023

Also published on the Embassy's previous website: zimbabweembassy.se/fake-registrar-general-offices-website-circulating-in-the-social-media/
