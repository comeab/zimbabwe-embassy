Welcome from the Ambassador

Welcome from the Ambassador

It is an honour to welcome you to the website for the Embassy of the Republic of Zimbabwe to the Kingdom of Sweden, Nordic and Baltic Countries.

This website is a valuable and educational portal, and I hope it will serve you as a useful resource, as well as a virtual connection to our homeland: an extension of Zimbabwean values and heritage, and a portal into our rich culture, trade and investment opportunities and tourist attractions.

H.E. Priscilla Misihairabwi-MushongaAmbassador of the Republic of Zimbabwe
