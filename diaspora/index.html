Diaspora

For Zimbabweans living in the Nordic and Baltic countries: notices, events, the community group and the annual Diaspora Conference.

Welcome to all Zimbabweans living in Sweden, Norway, Denmark, Finland, Estonia, Latvia and Lithuania. The Embassy is here to keep you connected with home, with each other and with what Zimbabwe is building. Contact the Embassy on any issue of interest to our country, and register your details below.

The Zimbabwe Diaspora Policy

The Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Trade leads the Government’s work with the diaspora. Its Diaspora Unit coordinates diaspora affairs and mobilises Zimbabweans abroad.

The Zimbabwe Diaspora Policy follows the philosophy of leaving no one and no place behind, and the idea that a nation is built by its own people (Nyika inovakwa nevene vayo, Ilizwe lakhiwa ngabanikazi balo), as Zimbabwe works towards upper-middle-income status by 2030. Through it, the Government recognises its responsibility for the interests and welfare of citizens living, studying and working abroad.

Stay informed

  • Notices for the diaspora, such as invitations, closures and warnings, are on the Notices page while they apply.
  • Events the Embassy organises or joins across the region are on the Agenda, and reports from past events are under News.
  • Join the community group below for news, invitations and help from fellow Zimbabweans.

Zimbabwe Annual Diaspora Conference

Every year the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Trade invites the diaspora to the Zimbabwe Annual Diaspora Conference. The 4th conference was held in Gweru, Midlands Province, from 9 to 11 April 2026, under the theme “Diaspora Powering Progress”. Read the invitation.

Supporting home

  • Donations. Before you send donations to Zimbabwe, read the guidelines on donations from the diaspora.
  • Investing. The diaspora can take part in Zimbabwe’s development through investment in many sectors. See Investment opportunities.

Consular services for the diaspora

Passports, birth registration for children born abroad, certification of documents and dual citizenship are all handled by the Embassy in Stockholm, by appointment.
